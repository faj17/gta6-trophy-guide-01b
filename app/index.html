    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions, in the order to play it. Story first, then strangers, then the long grinds — and the gold “A Long Road” (100% completion) last, just before the platinum.

    1. Stage 1 — The story, in order. Opening mission Gone Fishing, then Chapter 1, Chapter 2, Chapter 3, Chapter 4, and the final mission. Named missions along the way: Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory. 13 trophies, incl. the gold for the finale.
    2. Stage 2 — Choices and partner moments, before the finale. Make story choices as they come (Actions Speak Louder), grab the partner trophies while your partner is with you (Hot Together, Retail Therapy, Personal Best), and if you fail a mission, retry it at once (Bad Luck, I Guess). Keep a save from before the final mission. 5 trophies — all flagged ⚑ MISSABLE.
    3. Stage 3 — Stranger strands. Finish the five named strands — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone — plus three more, for 8 strands total across Leonida. 6 trophies, incl. the silver Strangers & Freaks.
    4. Stage 4 — Activities. Try all 9 activity types once; win 20 different races; win a Top Fun drone race at all 14 locations; gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges; beat Cal at fishing; finish an arcade game; bowl at Kolt & Associates. 7 trophies, incl. the silver The Shootist.
    5. Stage 5 — Exploration & collectibles. Visit all 52 areas; meditate at all 18 spots; WAiNK It at 100 locations; complete 3 card sets; discover 100 vehicles; buy all 8 garages; register 10 vehicles to Scooter Bros. 7 trophies, incl. the silver You WAiNKer!
    6. Stage 6 — Crime & money. Rob 5 bank vaults and escape; rob an armored truck; rob one illegal operation of each of the 9 gangs; rob a jewelry store undetected; launder $250,000 of Marked Bills; spend $100,000 in shops. 6 trophies, incl. the silver Maximum Withdrawal.
    7. Stage 7 — Combat & stunts. 250 stunts; 5 enemies in one Combat Focus; an unscheduled departure at VCIA; the Rhino; a melee drive-by; a strange encounter; 50 challenges. 7 trophies, incl. the silver Cunning Stunts.
    8. Stage 8 — The finish. Reach 100% completion for the gold A Long Road, and the platinum Vice Got a Price! pops with it. 2 trophies.

    ⚑ MISSABLE means the trophy is tied to a story chapter, a named mission, a story choice or a partner moment that may not come back — do it when the game offers it, and keep a manual save before big story beats. Count trophies (250 stunts, 100 WAiNK, 100 vehicles…) are grinds, not missables: they keep counting in free roam. Online: none of the 55 trophies is online — filter “Online” shows an empty list on purpose.
